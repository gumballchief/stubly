KEEPING ITSave this or you lose the wallet

Your wallet is tied to the Account ID on the card. Your PIN unlocks it — but only on a browser that knows that ID. Clear your browser data without saving it and the wallet becomes unreachable.

“Start a fresh wallet” doesn't delete the old one — it just stops using it here. You can always come back with the ID.

BE CLEAR ABOUT THISWhat a PIN wallet is, and isn't

No seed phrase

There isn't one, and that's deliberate. Circle splits the key into fragments held by separate parties, so a single secret phrase never exists. Nothing to write down, nothing to have stolen off a sticky note.

You can't export it

Because there's no single private key, this wallet cannot be imported into MetaMask or any other wallet. You can still send every cent out with your PIN, to any address, on Arc or through Circle's bridge.

If you forget the PIN

Circle asks the security questions you set when you created it. Three wrong tries locks you out for thirty minutes — that's Circle's rule, not ours.

It's yours, not ours

Stubly holds no balance for you. The USDC sits in your own Circle wallet, and nothing leaves it unless you type your PIN. Anyone holding your Account ID still needs that PIN.
