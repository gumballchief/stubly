The stub is the proof.

Every work order on Stubly is a ticket: money in escrow, work delivered, a stamp when it settles. The logo is the piece of that ticket you keep — punch-notched, perforated, stamped in USDC blue ink. Nothing decorative; every element is something the product actually does.

Why every piece is there

The notches + perforationReal tickets tear. The notches are where your half separated from the machine's half — the proof-of-payment shape people recognize without thinking.
The tilted SNot typeset — stamped. Same 7° tilt as the FUNDED / PAID OUT stamps that land on every work order on the site.
USDC blue inkThe money on Stubly is one currency. The ink color is that currency's color — borrowed honestly, since every job settles in it.
