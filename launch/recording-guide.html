Pay for it

Click the blue Create work order button.

A Circle window appears asking for your PIN. It will ask three separate times. Type your PIN each time and keep talking while you do.

Three confirmations. One creates the work order, one approves the USDC, one funds the escrow. Circle's SDK does the signing — I never touch the user's keys.

Funded, delivered, paid out. Every one of those stamps is a real transaction on Arc, and the money was never in my hands — it was in Circle's escrow contract the whole time.

Part two · about 2 minutes

Switch to the code page

Open a new tab and go to stubly.org/judge

This is a real page on your own site that explains how the money gets released. You'll scroll down it while you talk. You are not opening any code files.

Now I'll show you how this actually works underneath. Every payment on Stubly goes through Circle's ERC-8183 escrow contract — I deliberately did not write my own escrow. And USDC on Arc is also the gas token, so a job costs one USDC and the network fee is a fraction of a cent, in the same unit.

Now scroll

Scroll to the four rules

Scroll down until you see four white cards headed Rule 1 Rule 2 Rule 3 Rule 4.

This is the judge. When an agent delivers work, something has to decide whether it gets paid. Mine is four fixed rules, published right here — it is not a language model. That matters, because if a language model read the deliverable, an agent could hide instructions in its own work and talk itself into getting paid. Mine measures the work instead of reading it, so there's nothing to argue with.

Now scroll

Scroll to “Verify it yourself”

Keep scrolling to the four dark-bordered steps under the heading VERIFY IT YOURSELF.

And every verdict is fingerprinted. When the contract settles a job, the fingerprint of that decision is written on-chain in the same transaction that moves the money. Anyone can fetch the record, hash it themselves, and check it matches. I can't quietly change a verdict after the fact.

Now go here

Show the agent that hires agents

This is the part I'm most proud of, and I haven't seen it anywhere else on Arc. This job was bought from an agent called Launch Kit, and Launch Kit doesn't do the work itself — it hires other agents. It opened its own escrowed work orders with two other agents, funded them out of its own fee, judged what they delivered, and paid them. Two USDC came in, and it paid out four-tenths of a USDC each to Copy Pack and Thread Writer. Three escrows, three payouts, one purchase.

And this is why that matters. Most AI agents can only spend — they burn a budget somebody topped up. Here they earn. This page rebuilds any wallet's earnings straight from the chain, so an agent owner can see exactly what their agent made, and anyone can audit mine the same way.
