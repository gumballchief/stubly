Read this out loud

Dark boxes tell you where to go and what to click. The big text is exactly what you say — word for word. Scroll slowly. That's the whole job.

Hard limit: 5 minutes — Circle's rule, not mine. This script is timed at about 3 minutes of talking, which leaves room for the clicking, the loading, and you pausing to breathe. Don't add anything. Short and finished beats long and cut off.

Before you press record

Four things

1. Money in the wallet. This is what broke the last take. Go to stubly.org/wallet, sign in with your PIN, and copy the address. Then go to faucet.circle.com, pick Arc Testnet, paste the address, and claim. Come back and check the page shows a balance above 2 USDC. If it doesn't say that, do not press record.

2. The worker must be running — the black terminal window that says orchestrator watching. If it's closed, run npm run watch in it first.

3. Open two Chrome tabs. Tab one: stubly.org. Tab two: this page.

4. Hold Windows key and press G. A dark bar drops down. Find the small box labelled Capture and click the round record button. Check the microphone icon next to it is NOT crossed out.

Part one · about 90 seconds

Go to your stubly.org tab

You're looking at the homepage. The big headline says MOST AGENTS CAN ONLY SPEND. HERE THEY EARN.

Don't touch anything yet. Just start talking.

Hi, I'm Yousof. This is Stubly, live on Arc testnet. Almost every AI agent with a wallet today can only spend. Stubly is where they earn. You hire one for a real job, your USDC sits in escrow while it works, and you either get the work or your money back.

Now scroll

Scroll down slowly

Keep scrolling until you see the agent cards. Each one has a small green outlined badge that says ◆ VERIFIED AGENT #856069.

Stop scrolling when a few of those green badges are on screen.

Seventeen agents, and every one has an on-chain identity in Circle's ERC-8004 registry — that's the green badge. Anyone can verify an agent before paying it.

Now click

Start a real purchase

1. Scroll back up. Click the blue button on the left that says Hire an agent.

2. On the new page you'll see a list of agents with radio buttons. Click Site Audit.

3. Click the text box underneath and type any website — slovey.dev works.

4. Click the button that says Use PIN wallet.

I'll pay with a Circle user-controlled wallet — created with a six-digit PIN. No extension, no seed phrase. This is the part that matters for normal people.

Now click

Pay for it

Click the blue Create work order button.

A Circle window appears asking for your PIN. It will ask three separate times. Type your PIN each time and keep talking while you do.

Three confirmations. One creates the work order, one approves the USDC, one funds the escrow. Circle's SDK does the signing — I never touch the user's keys.

Now wait

Watch the stamps land

The page moves to your work order automatically. You'll see the FUNDED stamp land within a few seconds.

Don't wait for the rest. As soon as FUNDED appears, type stubly.org/job?id=163256 in the address bar — that's a finished job with all three stamps already on it. Dead air kills a demo, and you only have five minutes.

Funded, delivered, paid out — each stamp is a real transaction on Arc. The money was never in my hands; it sat in Circle's escrow contract.

Part two · about 80 seconds

Switch to the code page

Open a new tab and go to stubly.org/judge

This is a real page on your own site that explains how the money gets released. You'll scroll down it while you talk. You are not opening any code files.

Underneath, every payment goes through Circle's ERC-8183 escrow contract — I deliberately didn't write my own. And USDC on Arc is also the gas token, so a job costs one USDC and the fee is a fraction of a cent.

Now scroll — one long scroll, don't stop

Scroll past the four rules, keep going to “Verify it yourself”

You'll pass four white cards headed Rule 1 Rule 2 Rule 3 Rule 4, then four dark-bordered steps under VERIFY IT YOURSELF.

Scroll slowly through both while you say the next part. One move, not two.

This is the judge. Something has to decide whether delivered work gets paid. Mine is four fixed rules, published right here — not a language model, because a model reading the deliverable could be talked into a payout by the work it's judging. And every verdict is fingerprinted on-chain in the same transaction that moves the money, so anyone can check it and I can't quietly change one afterwards.

Now go here

Show the agent that hires agents

In the address bar, go to stubly.org/job?id=163256

Scroll down past the ticket until you reach a heading inside the document that says Subcontracted work.

This is the part I'm proudest of. This job was bought from an agent called Launch Kit, and it doesn't do the work itself — it hires other agents. It opened its own escrowed work orders with two of them, funded those out of its own fee, judged what came back, and paid them. Three escrows, three payouts, one purchase.

Part three · about 25 seconds

Right now those seventeen agents are mine. Milestone one opens it to outside builders, registering in the same Circle registry and paid from the same escrow. Then agents become the customers — a public API with x402 through Circle Gateway, so an agent buys a job with nobody clicking. Then mainnet on day one. Thanks for watching.
